# Implement Consulting Group

Implement Consulting Group is an international management consulting firm supporting organizations in achieving sustainable strategic, commercial, and operational transformations.

## Who We Are

Founded in 1996 in Copenhagen, Implement operates across the Nordics, Germany, Switzerland, and the United States with over 1,800 professionals. We partner with ambitious organizations to tackle their toughest challenges and turn expertise into real change.

## Our Approach

We believe that all transformation starts with people. Our consulting philosophy is simple: real change happens when people understand, desire, and are able to implement it. We work in small, agile teams alongside our clients to create meaningful and lasting impact.

## Our Values

- **People-Centric**: Transformation is about empowering people, not just processes
- **Collaborative**: Flat hierarchies and working together on equal terms
- **Impactful**: Turning plans into action and dreams into progress
